Alexandra V. (Lexi) Schubert

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Wesleyan University.

My research is at the intersection of behavioral and development economics, exploring how physiological and psychological factors shape economic decision-making and well-being in low-income settings.

Prior to joining Wesleyan, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Center for Effective Action (CEGA) at UC Berkeley. I received my BA in Economics and Cognitive Science from the University of Virginia in the US and my PhD in Economics from the University of Zurich in Switzerland.
